## Team meetings

- **Daily stand-up:** 10:15 AM IST, 15 minutes.
- **Bug triage:** Tuesday and Friday, 3 PM IST.
- **Sprint planning:** first Monday of each sprint (sprints are 2 weeks long).
- **Release retrospective:** the Friday after each release, 30 minutes.

## Communication channels

- **#tt-qa**: daily QA discussion and nightly run summaries
- **#tt-release**: release status and Critical bug alerts
- **#tt-devops**: environment problems

## Escalation path

1. Talk to the module owner.
2. If still blocked after 4 hours, escalate to the QA Lead.
3. For release-blocking problems, the QA Lead escalates to the Release Manager.
